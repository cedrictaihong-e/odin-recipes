Delicious cake!

Cake is by far my favourite dessert. This recipe will make your mouth water!

Hope you enjoy this recipe!

Ingredients:

  • flour
  • sugar
  • eggs (2)
  • cocoa powder (1 tablespoon)
  • frosting

Steps:

  1. mix 2 cups of flour with 1 cup sugar, 2 eggs & 1 tablespoon of cocoa powder
  2. Add cooking oil to mixture & mix till it becomes thick & fudgy.
  3. Place it in an oiled cake pan.
  4. Bake till cooked through at 400 degrees F
  5. Add your favourite frosting!

Enjoy!
