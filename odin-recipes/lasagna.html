Lasagna recipe

Lasagna has been my favourite food for as long as I can remember, so I'm gonna share the recipe with you today!

I hope you enjoy this recipe!

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna Noodles
  • Salted water
  • Hamburger (1 pound)
  • Cheese
  • Pasta Sauce

Steps:

  1. Boil a sufficient amount of lasagna noodles in salted water to cover your pan
  2. Cook 1 pound of hamburger in a pan till brown
  3. Shred your preferred amount of cheese
  4. Once the noodles & hamburger are ready, begin layering the pan the following way over & over till the pan is filled:
    1. Noodles
    2. Meat
    3. Pasta sauce
    4. Cheese
  5. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes, 30 minutes uncovered & 15 minutes covered

Enjoy!
